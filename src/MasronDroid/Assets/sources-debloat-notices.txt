Debloat catalog sources
UAD-NG: https://github.com/Universal-Debloater-Alliance/universal-android-debloater-next-generation/blob/69ca5269151ab0788eae7af851090702974a9153/resources/assets/uad_lists.json
UAD Classic: https://github.com/0x192/universal-android-debloater/blob/11f27c671cba278d71296cdef4c5a5dba06add5e/resources/assets/uad_lists.json
The projects share ancestry. MasronDroid joins their package IDs, retains the higher risk when ratings differ, and prefers the NG description. The compressed JSON contains the editable catalog. Upstream license texts accompany this file.
App Manager: https://github.com/MuntashirAkon/AppManager/blob/0d1d926a858c0236d6c2aed0c434992d4187f3a5/app/src/main/assets/debloat.json
Xiaomi Debloater: https://github.com/fadeltd/xiaomi-debloater/blob/da8a21c4b1e163dafff3a97e80491a0ef1642f8a/bloatware.json (Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO only). Some sources reuse UAD. Conflicts retain the highest applicable risk.
Prune brand package data: https://github.com/26August/Prune/tree/462e8dc9696d8564f27f59414875c8658afb3608/app/src/main/assets/packages . Brand-specific overlays retain upstream verification flags; optional and unverified safe entries are treated as at least Caution.
